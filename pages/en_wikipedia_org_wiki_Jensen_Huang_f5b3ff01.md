**Jen-Hsun**"**Jensen**"**Huang**[[a]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-3) (born February 17, 1963) is a Taiwanese and American  electrical engineer and businessman. He co-founded the technology company [Nvidia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia "Nvidia"), one of the world's [most valuable corporations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_public_corporations_by_market_capitalization "List of public corporations by market capitalization"), and served as its president and CEO from its founding in 1993 until his resignation in 2026. As of November 2026,_[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")_ estimates his net worth at US$128.4 billion, making him the [14th-wealthiest person](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World's_Billionaires "The World's Billionaires") in the world.

Born in [Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei "Taipei"), Huang spent his childhood in Taiwan and Thailand before immigrating to the United States. After earning a master's degree from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University "Stanford University"), he launched Nvidia alongside [Chris Malachowsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Malachowsky "Chris Malachowsky") and [Curtis Priem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Priem "Curtis Priem") from a [Denny's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denny's "Denny's") restaurant in [San Jose, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose,_California "San Jose, California"), in 1993. Huang led the company out of near-bankruptcy during the 1990s and oversaw its expansion from [graphics processing unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphics_processing_unit "Graphics processing unit") (GPU) development into [high-performance computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-performance_computing "High-performance computing") and [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence") (AI).

Under Huang's leadership, Nvidia experienced massive growth during the [AI boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_boom "AI boom"), becoming the first company to reach a [market capitalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_capitalization "Market capitalization") of more than $5 trillion in October 2025. In 2021 and 2024, _[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time\_(magazine) "Time (magazine)")_ magazine included him in [its list of the most influential people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100 "Time 100"), and later named him as one of the "Architects of AI" for its 2025 [Person of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_Person_of_the_Year "Time Person of the Year"). He was also awarded the [IEEE Medal of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Medal_of_Honor "IEEE Medal of Honor") and the [National Medal of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Medal_of_Science "National Medal of Science") in 2026 for his contributions to [machine learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning "Machine learning") and [computational science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_science "Computational science").

In November 2026, it was reported that [Huang had had multiple extramarital affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang_scandal "Jensen Huang scandal"), including one with Chinese business executive [Alice Huang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_Huang_(executive) "Alice Huang (executive)").[[b]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-4) The U.S. government initially suspected that she may have been a [spy for China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_espionage_in_the_United_States "Chinese espionage in the United States"); later reporting disputed that there was conclusive evidence of this. As a result, Huang resigned from his positions at Nvidia, and the company confirmed that he would have no formal role at the firm going forward.

## Early life and education

Huang was born in [Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei "Taipei"), Taiwan, on February 17, 1963,[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:9-5) and moved to the southern city of [Tainan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tainan "Tainan") as a child.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:2-6) He is the second son of Huang Hsing-tai,[[c]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-7) a [chemical engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_engineer "Chemical engineer") at an [oil refinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_refinery "Oil refinery"), and Lo Tsai-hsiu,[[d]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-8)a schoolteacher.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:10-9) They were a middle-class Taiwanese family that relocated often,[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202417-10) and were native speakers of [Taiwanese Hokkien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Hokkien "Taiwanese Hokkien").[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEWitt20255-11) Each day, Jensen's mother randomly selected 10 words from the dictionary to teach her sons English.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202417%E2%80%9318-12) When he was five years old, Huang's family moved to [Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand "Thailand") to support his father's refinery career and remained there for approximately four years.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:2-6)[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Lin-13) He attended [Ruamrudee International School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruamrudee_International_School "Ruamrudee International School") while in [Bangkok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok "Bangkok").[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-14)

In the late 1960s, Hsing-tai traveled from [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan "Taiwan") to [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City") to train under an [air conditioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_conditioning "Air conditioning") company and, after returning home, resolved to send his sons to the United States.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-15) When he was nine years old, Jensen's parents sent him to live in the United States, even though he did not yet speak English fluently.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-16) He and his older brother moved in 1973 to live with an uncle in [Tacoma, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacoma,_Washington "Tacoma, Washington").[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:3-17) Both Huang's aunt and uncle were recent immigrants to [Washington state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_(state) "Washington (state)"); they accidentally enrolled him and his brother in the [Oneida Baptist Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oneida_Baptist_Institute "Oneida Baptist Institute"), a religious reform academy in [Oneida, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oneida,_Kentucky "Oneida, Kentucky"), for troubled youth,[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:3-17) mistakenly believing it to be a prestigious boarding school.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) In order to afford the academy's tuition, Jensen's parents sold nearly all their possessions.[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202418-19)

At the age of 10, Huang lived with his older brother in the boys' dormitory at Oneida Institute.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) Because he was too young to attend classes there, Huang enrolled at Oneida Elementary School, a public school in town, arriving as "an undersized Asian immigrant with long hair and heavily accented English".[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) He was frequently bullied and beaten by classmates.[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202418%E2%80%9319-20) In Oneida, Huang cleaned toilets every day, learned to play [table tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Table_tennis "Table tennis"),[[e]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-22) joined a swimming team,[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-23) and appeared in _[Sports Illustrated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports\_Illustrated "Sports Illustrated")_ at age 14.[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:4-24) He taught his roommate how to read in exchange for being taught how to [bench press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bench_press "Bench press").[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18)[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:4-24) In 2002, Huang said he remembered the part of his life in Kentucky "more vividly than just about any other".[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:4-24)

Two years after Huang arrived in Oneida, his parents moved to the United States and settled in [Beaverton, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaverton,_Oregon "Beaverton, Oregon"), after which the brothers withdrew from school in Kentucky to live with them.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202419-25) As a teenager, Huang attended [Aloha High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aloha_High_School "Aloha High School") in [Aloha, Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aloha,_Oregon "Aloha, Oregon"),[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-26) where he excelled academically. He [skipped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grade_skipping "Grade skipping") two grades, graduated at age 16, and became a nationally ranked table-tennis player, in addition to being a member of its mathematics, computer, and science clubs.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) In 1977, the school purchased an [Apple II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_II "Apple II") computer which Huang used to play [_Super Star Trek_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_(1971_video_game) "Star Trek (1971 video game)"), a text-based video game, and to program in [BASIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BASIC "BASIC"), creating his own version of [Snake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snake_(video_game_genre) "Snake (video game genre)").[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEWitt202528-27)

At the age of 15, Huang's first job was working the [graveyard shift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shift_plan#Graveyard_shift "Shift plan")[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:5-28) at a local [Denny's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denny's "Denny's") restaurant as a dishwasher, [busboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busser "Busser"), and waiter from 1978 to 1983.[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-29)[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:6-30)[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:7-31) After high school, he enrolled at [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University "Oregon State University") because of its low tuition fees.[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202421-32) He studied [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering "Electrical engineering") and graduated in 1984 with a bachelor's degree with highest honors.[[28]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-33)[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEWitt202512-34) Huang later recalled, "I was the youngest kid in school, in class" and the only student who "looked like a child".[[30]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-35) While working as a microchip designer in [Silicon Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley "Silicon Valley"), he took graduate classes at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University "Stanford University"), where he earned a master's degree in electrical engineering in 1992.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18)[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-36)

## AMD and LSI Logic

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sun_GX_version_1.jpg)

A Sun GX version 1. Note the two large LSI Logic chips implementing the accelerator.

After college, Huang worked as a microchip designer in [Silicon Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley "Silicon Valley").[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) He was approached for positions at [Texas Instruments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Instruments "Texas Instruments"), [Advanced Micro Devices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMD "AMD") (AMD), and [LSI Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LSI_Logic "LSI Logic"),[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-37) ultimately choosing the California-based AMD due to already being familiar with the company. From 1984 to 1985,[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Lee1-38) Huang designed AMD [microprocessors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microprocessor "Microprocessor") while simultaneously attending Stanford and raising his two children. However, when he heard of new chip design processes at LSI Logic, Huang left AMD to assume a role as a technical officer at LSI Logic, working under a [startup company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Startup_company "Startup company"), [Sun Microsystems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Microsystems "Sun Microsystems"), where he met engineers [Chris Malachowsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Malachowsky "Chris Malachowsky") and [Curtis Priem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Priem "Curtis Priem").[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202422%E2%80%9323-39)

LSI was in contract with Sun Microsystems and had introduced Huang to Malachowsky and Priem, who were working on a new [graphics accelerator card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphics_card "Graphics card"). While the three produced the card's manufacturing process, the relationship between Malachowsky and Priem became strained as the two disputed the chip's design, leading to infighting; according to Malachowsky, they "broke every tool that LSI Logic had in their standard portfolio".[[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202433%E2%80%9334-40)

In 1989, Huang, Malachowsky, and Priem finalized the accelerator, which they called the "GX graphics engine".[[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202434-41) GX was a widespread financial success; the sales of the graphics engine contributed to Sun Microsystems' revenue increasing from $262 million in 1987 to $656 million in 1990, and Huang was promoted to be the director of LSI's CoreWare, a division that manufactured chips for hardware vendors.[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202436%E2%80%9337-42) He stayed at LSI until 1993.[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Lee1-38)

## Nvidia

### Founding (1993)

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:2484_Berryessa_Road.jpg)

Huang co-founded [Nvidia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia "Nvidia") Corporation after meeting with [Chris Malachowsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Malachowsky "Chris Malachowsky") and [Curtis Priem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Priem "Curtis Priem") in a [Denny's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denny's "Denny's") diner in [East San Jose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_San_Jose "East San Jose") (pictured). As a teenager, Huang was an employee at a Denny's restaurant in Oregon.

When business began to slow for Sun Microsystems after 1990, Huang, alongside Priem and Malachowsky, resigned to found a venture making [graphics chips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_display_controller "Video display controller") for [PC games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PC_game "PC game").[[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202439%E2%80%9343-43) They initially named their new company "NVision" until Huang suggested that the company be named based on the Latin word _[invidia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invidia "Invidia")_, as Priem wanted competitors to turn "green with envy".[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) They eventually dropped the "i" to honor the NV1 chip that they were then developing.[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:0-44) The three met frequently in 1992 at a Denny's roadside diner in [East San Jose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_San_Jose "East San Jose") to formulate a [business plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_plan "Business plan").[[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202441-45) Huang chose for them to meet at Denny's due to his prior work experience at the restaurant chain and because it was "quieter than home and had cheap coffee".[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) The three founded the company during one meeting at a breakfast booth at the diner.[[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-46)[[42]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Whitaker-47)

To formally incorporate the company, Huang found a lawyer, James Gaither of [Cooley Godward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooley_LLP "Cooley LLP"), who used $200 of Huang's money to capitalize the company.[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-:0-44) After that meeting, Huang went back to Priem and Malachowsky to ask each of them for $200 for their respective shares of the company, which meant that Nvidia's initial capital was $600.[[43]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202443-48) On April 5, 1993, Huang personally signed Nvidia's original [articles of incorporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Articles_of_association "Articles of association") into effect.[[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-NVIDIA_Articles-49)

Although he had left LSI, Huang remained in good standing with the company and was able to secure funding for Nvidia from LSI's CEO, [Wilfred Corrigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilfred_Corrigan "Wilfred Corrigan"), who introduced Huang to venture capitalist [Don Valentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Valentine "Don Valentine").[[45]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEWitt202531-50) Valentine, the leader of [Sequoia Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequoia_Capital "Sequoia Capital"), chose to invest in Nvidia through Corrigan's support,[[45]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEWitt202531-50) as did [Sutter Hill Ventures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutter_Hill_Ventures "Sutter Hill Ventures"). The funding enabled Nvidia to begin development efforts toward its first chip and to begin paying wages for its employees.[[46]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202447%E2%80%9352,_54-51) By the first day of operation, Huang was made Nvidia's president and CEO.[[47]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202454%E2%80%9355-52) Even though Huang, at age 30, was younger than Priem and Malachowsky, both Priem and Malachowsky believed that he was prepared to be CEO.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) According to Priem, "we basically deferred to Jensen on day one" and told Huang, "you're in charge of running the company—all the stuff Chris and I don't know how to do".[[48]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKim202455-53)[[f]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-54)

### President and CEO (1993–2026)

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Jensen_Huang_at_Computex_Taipei_20160531a.jpg)

Huang at [Computex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computex "Computex") Taipei in 2016

By 2024, Huang had been Nvidia's chief executive for over three decades, a tenure described by _[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\_Wall\_Street\_Journal "The Wall Street Journal")_ as "almost unheard of in fast-moving [Silicon Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley "Silicon Valley")".[[49]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-fitch20240226-55) He owns 3.6% of Nvidia's stock, which went public in 1999.[[50]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Forbes-56) He earned US$24.6 million as CEO in 2007, ranking him as the 61st highest paid U.S. CEO by _[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes "Forbes")_.[[50]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Forbes-56) According to Huang, the three co-founders in 1993 had "no idea how" to start a company,[[42]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Whitaker-47) "building Nvidia turned out to have been a million times harder" than they expected, and they probably would not have done it if they had realized up front "the pain and suffering [involved] ... the challenges [they were] going to endure, the embarrassment and the shame, and the list of all the things that [would] go wrong."[[51]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Taylor-57)

For its first graphics accelerator chips, Nvidia focused on rendering [quadrilateral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrilateral "Quadrilateral")[primitives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_primitive "Geometric primitive") ([forward texture mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_texture_mapping "Forward texture mapping")) instead of the triangle primitives preferred by its competitors,[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) and barely survived long enough to successfully pivot to triangles only because [Sega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sega "Sega") agreed to keep Nvidia alive with a $5 million investment.[[52]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Cohen-58) By the time the [RIVA 128](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIVA_128 "RIVA 128") was released in August 1997 and saved the company, Nvidia was down to one month of payroll.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) This resulted in the "unofficial company motto": "Our company is thirty days from going out of business."[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) Huang regularly began presentations to Nvidia staff with those words for many years.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Witt-18) However, Huang regards the "pain and suffering" of Nvidia's early years as essential to the company's success in later years, because it forced him to become a better leader.[[53]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Huddleston-59)

[](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:JensenHuangSC18.jpg)

Huang did not keep a fixed office. He roamed Nvidia's headquarters and settled temporarily in conference rooms as needed.[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Nusca-60) He has said he does not wear a watch, because he believes that "now is the most important time".[[49]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-fitch20240226-55) He preferred to maintain a relatively [flat management structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat_organization "Flat organization"), with around 60 direct reports as of November 2024,[[55]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Kahn-61) based upon his view that people reporting directly to him "should be at the top of their game" and "require the least amount of pampering".[[56]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-62)

His management style has also been characterized as abusive and belittling by employees, with Huang making graphic threats and ridiculing subordinates in front of colleagues or clients.[[57]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-SteelHuangMacMickle2026-1-63) In 2005, Nvidia terminated a vice president of human resources, Michael Winston, who objected to Huang's behavior. In response to the threat of legal action, Nvidia later settled with Winston for over a million dollars, though the company denied wrongdoing.[[57]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-SteelHuangMacMickle2026-1-63)

Historically, Huang and Nvidia were well-known only among the [gamers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamer "Gamer") and [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics "Computer graphics") experts who were the original intended markets for Nvidia's [graphics processing unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphics_processing_unit "Graphics processing unit") (GPU) products. In 2017, a _[Fortune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortune\_(magazine) "Fortune (magazine)")_ profile article suggested: "If you haven't heard of Nvidia, you can be forgiven."[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Nusca-60) During the [AI boom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_boom "AI boom"), Huang's net worth rose rapidly along with the value of Nvidia's stock, from US$3 billion in 2019 to US$90 billion in May 2024.[[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Leswing-64) During the same timeframe, Huang became more widely known. In March 2024, [Mark Zuckerberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Zuckerberg "Mark Zuckerberg") wrote on [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "Instagram") with a picture of himself and Huang wearing each other's signature jacket: "He's like [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift "Taylor Swift"), but for tech".[[59]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#cite_note-Tan-65)
